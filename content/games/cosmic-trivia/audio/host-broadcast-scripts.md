# Cosmic Trivia Host Broadcast Scripts

These scripts are split by director phase and written for a single upbeat host voice: clear, friendly, and not overly chatty.

## `interest-selecting`

Main:

> Welcome to Cosmic Trivia... Please choose your favorite categories and keywords on your phone... The better you pick... the better the questions will match you.

Short:

> Pick your categories and keywords... We'll start as soon as you're ready.

## `preferences-locked`

Main:

> Your selections are locked in... The deck is being prepared now... Stay tuned... the first round is about to begin.

Short:

> Preferences confirmed. We're moving into the question deck now.

## `deck-loading`

Main:

> We are drawing your questions now... One moment while we line up the best questions for this round.

Short:

> Loading the deck. Please wait a moment.

## `question-intro`

Main:

> Get ready... Here comes the next question.
> Alright... let's bring up the next question.
> Here we go... next question on deck.

Short:

> Listen closely.
> Next question.

## `question-audio`

Main:

> No host voice here. Play the question title audio instead.

Short:

> Question title only.

## `answering`

Main:

> Answer now... Submit your choice on your phone before time runs out.
> Take your time... but don't wait too long.
> Lock in your answer on your phone... before the clock runs out.

Short:

> Time to answer. Don't miss the clock.
> Make your pick.

## `scoring`

Main:

> Scores are in... Let's see who is leading this round.
> Points are moving... let's see the leaderboard shift.
> Nice work... the board is updating now.
> Nobody got it right... that one was a tough one.
> No one nailed it... wow, that question was sneaky.
> Looks like this round stumped everyone.

Short:

> Scores updated. The leaderboard is moving.
> Let's see the board.

## `next-question`

Main:

> Next question coming up... Keep the momentum going.
> Let's keep rolling... next question coming up.
> Up next... another question.

Short:

> The next question is almost here.
> Next one up.

## `complete`

Main:

> That's the end of the game... Congratulations to our top player... and thank you for playing Cosmic Trivia. We'll see you next time.

Short:

> Game over. We have a champion. Thanks for playing.

## Recording notes

- Use the existing test audio `phase-interest-selecting-choose-preferences-01.mp3` for `interest-selecting`.
- For `question-audio`, prefer the question-specific `questionAudio` file.
- If you want a stronger host presence, record two takes for `answering` and `complete`: one calm, one more energetic.
